What would strengthen IbogaBase

LAST REVIEWED 2026-09-22 · HONEST GAPS · NOT A RANKING CLAIM

IbogaBase is a living educational reference. More pages alone do not make it stronger. This page lists concrete gaps that still limit confidence. It is not a claim that the site is best-in-field, complete, or peer-endorsed.

Named reviewers — still open (red)

Independent named review seats are not filled. No medical, cultural, or legal reviewer is listed as having accepted scope-limited review. Until that changes, pages should be read as source-compiled public reference, not expert endorsement. Outreach is paused pending owner decision — this page does not invite or contact reviewers.

  • Medical / toxicology — mortality and adverse-event language bounds.
  • Cardiology (QT/QTc-aware) — cardiac-risk framing without under- or over-statement.
  • Addiction psychiatry / medicine — OUD/AUD evidence grades vs media cure language.
  • Trauma / PTSD research — veteran-media claims vs observational study limits.
  • Gabonese / Bwiti cultural steward–scholar — public-vs-private knowledge boundary (consent-first).
  • Drug-policy / controlled-substances law — Schedule I vs state-bill accuracy; not legal advice.
  • Librarian / information scientist — citation hygiene and catalog discipline.

Related process notes live on Standards and Methodology. Reviewer names appear only with consent.

Evidence and safety gaps

  • Many condition pages remain observational- or media-heavy; randomized clinical evidence for ibogaine in humans is still limited.
  • Mortality denominators and clinic-reported “success rates” need ongoing source discipline — numerators without denominators stay weak.
  • DIY / self-treatment refusal is policy; the site will not add protocols, dosing, or referrals to close a “coverage” gap.

Catalog and link hygiene

  • Listening Library: one outbound URL remains dead-flagged (Clare Wilkins / ibogaineconference.org). No invented replacement.
  • Full sources catalog inline expansion vs dedicated citation-hygiene pages — prefer verified citations over volume.
  • Trial registry rows should stay tied to working ClinicalTrials.gov NCT links and labeled status dates.

See Listening Library verify status, Trials, and Citation hygiene.

Culture and law gaps

  • Culture desk coverage from outsider secondary sources cannot substitute for steward review of public-boundary language.
  • State-bill trackers need periodic re-check against primary bill text; funding politics ≠ Schedule I repeal or FDA approval.

What this page is not

  • Not a claim of best-in-field status or Jev ≥ 3.5.
  • Not medical advice, legal advice, dosing guidance, or clinic referral.
  • Not a reviewer call-for-proposals or outreach list for public contact.
