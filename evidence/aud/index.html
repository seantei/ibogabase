Evidence · condition

Alcohol use disorder

LAST REVIEWED 2026-09-22 · PILOT / OBSERVATIONAL · NOT PROVEN

AUD human evidence for ibogaine remains early. Pilot open-label and observational program reports exist; they are not FDA approval and not a reason to self-treat.

Evidence: Very limitedNot provenNo dosing

Is ibogaine proven for alcohol use disorder?

No — not established. An IND or early-phase noribogaine research action is not AUD approval.

Very limited human evidence
