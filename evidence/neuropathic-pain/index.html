Neuropathic pain

LAST REVIEWED 2026-09-22 · VERY LIMITED EVIDENCE

Ibogaine is not established as a proven treatment for neuropathic pain. This page exists so media and book themes have an honest evidence-grade landing URL.

Evidence: Very limited Not proven Media anecdotes labeled

Is ibogaine proven for neuropathic pain?

Not established. Above-fold posture: very limited evidence / not proven. Do not upgrade book or podcast anecdotes into clinical proof.

Evidence level
Very limited — far thinner than OUD observational literature
Source kinds often cited in discourse
Case-level reports, animal/mechanistic speculation, and media/book anecdotes (label as media until primary URLs are confirmed)
What is not shown
Adequately powered controlled evidence that ibogaine treats neuropathic pain as a general indication
Safety
Cardiac / QT themes still apply whenever ibogaine is discussed — Safety / QTc
Self-treatment
Out of scope — DIY refusal

How does this differ from OUD / TBI literature density?

OUD and TBI pages at least have named observational cohorts and registered research to discuss with design limits. Neuropathic pain does not currently match that density on IbogaBase. Contrast: OUD, TBI.

FAQ

Did a book or podcast prove remyelination / pain cure?

Media and book claims stay media-labeled until primary peer-reviewed sources are cited on this page. Miracle remyelination language is not IbogaBase voice.

Where should writers add citations next?

Only after opening primary URLs; do not invent trials. Corrections welcome via Changes.
