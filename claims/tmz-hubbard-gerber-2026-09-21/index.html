TMZ / Hubbard on Gerber — “isn’t miracle cure” (observatory)

LAST REVIEWED 2026-09-22 · MEDIA OBSERVATORY ONLY · NOT AMPLIFICATION

A short media-observatory card for a 2026-09-21 TMZ piece quoting Bryan Hubbard in the Presley Gerber news cycle: title/lead stress that ibogaine “isn’t [a] miracle cure,” alongside cardiac-arrest context in related TMZ coverage. Labeled media; not a primary safety study.

Media / anecdote Observatory only No clinic referral

What was published?

Source (media): TMZ, 2026-09-21 — “Presley Gerber Death: Ibogaine Activist Says Drug Can Save Lives, Isn’t Miracle Cure” (outbound link). OG description frames Gerber’s reported prior ibogaine use and quotes an advocate saying it is “not as simple as taking a magic pill.” The piece identifies Bryan Hubbard (Americans for Ibogaine) on “TMZ Live,” and related TMZ reporting in the same news cycle discusses cardiac arrest / deferred cause of death — still media, not autopsy adjudication by IbogaBase.

Why this card exists: To log a high-visibility media statement that explicitly rejects miracle-cure framing, while keeping cardiac-risk context visible. IbogaBase does not amplify celebrity narrative, diagnose Gerber’s death, or recommend any product or clinic.

Verdict

Media observatory — not primary evidence. “Isn’t miracle cure” language from an advocate in entertainment media is consistent with IbogaBase’s refusal of miracle/cure voice, but it is still advocacy + journalism. Cardiac-arrest mentions in the same news cycle are context labels, not a substitute for forensic or peer-reviewed causality analysis.

What we do not do with this item

  • No dosing, no self-treatment tips, no clinic referrals.
  • No claim that Hubbard’s advocacy proves or disproves efficacy.
  • No speculation beyond what the linked media pages state.
