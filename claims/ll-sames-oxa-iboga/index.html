Testable claims extracted (media → primary)

  1. Claim (paper title / abstract framing): Oxa-iboga alkaloids “lack cardiac risk” relative to ibogaine in the assays reported, and disrupt opioid use in animal models.
    Primary: Havel, Kruegel, … Sames et al., “Oxa-Iboga alkaloids lack cardiac risk and disrupt opioid use in animal models,” Nature Communications 2024;15:8118. PMID 39304653, DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-51856-y. Published online 2024-09-20 (Crossref).
  2. Claim (media extension risk): That animal cardiac-assay results mean human ibogaine or oxa-analogs are “safe” or clinically ready.
    Verdict: Not established. Animal / in-vitro cardiac risk reduction ≠ human approval, dosing guidance, or Schedule I status change. Keep QTc and noribogaine / analogs desks separate.
  3. Claim (taxonomy): Oxa-iboga species are interchangeable with plant iboga or clinical ibogaine hydrochloride.
    Verdict: False for literacy purposes — see Compare.

Verdict

Strong preclinical / chemistry signal; not a human efficacy or safety approval. Use the interview to locate the paper; do not upgrade podcast enthusiasm into “best drug” or miracle language (IbogaBase refuses best-in-field claims).
