Testable claims extracted (media → primary)

  1. Claim (political / media): Ibogaine is an established PTSD treatment ready for broad use.
    Evidence desk: PTSD evidence — limited observational / secondary signals; not proven as PTSD standard care.
  2. Claim (veteran study stream): Nature Medicine magnesium–ibogaine work proves PTSD cure.
    Primary: Cherian et al. PMID 38182784 (open-label TBI-focused cohort with secondary symptom domains) + critique PMID 42330793.
  3. Claim (approval): Political endorsement equals FDA approval of ibogaine for PTSD.
    Verdict: False — FDA check + Schedule I FAQ.

Verdict

Valuable skeptical/policy media for overclaim detection; still not a primary trial. Keep PTSD and TBI pages on observational limits and refuse miracle language.
