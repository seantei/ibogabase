Wanted sources

These are the gaps that would most improve the public reference.

  • Named medical reviewers: Cardiology, addiction medicine, emergency medicine, pharmacology, and psychiatry reviewers who are willing to be listed.
  • Named cultural advisors: Gabonese, Bwiti/Bwete-linked, conservation, and stewardship advisors only when they consent to public naming.
  • Adverse-event case material: Peer-reviewed case reports, agency records, and serious journalism with enough detail to separate signal from speculation.
  • State-policy primary records: Bill text, fiscal notes, committee reports, hearing video, agency pages, and final signed law when available.
  • Primary media links: Original podcast, YouTube, documentary, book, and interview pages rather than reposted summaries.

How to read the labels

Source type says what kind of record this is: media, law, study, trial, culture, claim, or review. Confidence describes how reliable the record is for identifying that source, not whether every claim inside it is true. The catalog is a finding aid.
