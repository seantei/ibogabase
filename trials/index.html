Clinical trials hub

Every registered human trial of ibogaine or noribogaine this catalog tracks, with phase, current registry status, enrollment, and sponsor. Statuses were pulled directly from ClinicalTrials.gov on 2026-09-22 (API v2). A registered trial is not evidence of safety or effectiveness — most listed studies are small, early-phase, or observational.

Registered trials

Click any registry ID to read the full official record. Note what is not here: no completed randomized efficacy trial of ibogaine exists. The atai Phase 1/2 study is the largest controlled program to date; its peer-reviewed results determine how much weight it can carry.

How to read this table

  • Registry status is what ClinicalTrials.gov reports (Recruiting, Completed, Withdrawn, Unknown, Active not recruiting). It is not a results verdict.
  • Completed means the study finished enrollment/follow-up as registered — not that peer-reviewed efficacy is established.
  • Analog rows (18-MC / NCT04292197, NCT03084952) are listed because they appear in ibogaine-adjacent registry search; they are not ibogaine trials. See analogs reference.
  • Trifecta note: NCT06810765 (Johns Hopkins, withdrawn) and NCT07717866 (Axial Therapeutic Research, active not recruiting) share the Trifecta name but are different registry records — do not merge them.
  • No dosing, no clinic referrals. Educational reference only.
