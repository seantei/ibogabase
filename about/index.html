About IbogaBase

IbogaBase is an educational reference. It helps readers inspect sources, understand uncertainty, and avoid hype.

IbogaBase is not legal advice and is not medical advice. It is an educational reference that links to original sources, summarizes in its own words, labels uncertainty, and keeps commercial relationships visible.

IbogaBase sources information: studies, laws, news, media, cultural context, safety literature, and claim notes. It does not source or sell Iboga, Ibogaine, clinics, treatment access, or provider referrals.

FDA permission for an investigational study is not FDA approval and not proof of safety or effectiveness.
