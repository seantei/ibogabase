Media

Media library

Podcasts, interviews, documentaries, books, videos, and source notes are listed as media sources, not clinical evidence.

Podcasts, interviews, documentaries, books, and news stories preserve public conversation and field history. They are media sources, not clinical evidence unless a claim is supported elsewhere. Media records link to original sources; audio, full transcripts, and long show notes are not rehosted.

How to read the labels

Source type says what kind of record this is: media, law, study, trial, culture, claim, or review. Confidence describes how reliable the record is for identifying that source, not whether every claim inside it is true. On this page, media means the item is useful for public conversation or field history; it is not clinical evidence by itself. The library now shows every indexed media record, not just recent items.
