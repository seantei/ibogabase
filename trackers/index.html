How to read the labels

Source type says what kind of record this is: media, law, study, trial, culture, claim, or review. Confidence describes how reliable the record is for identifying that source, not whether every claim inside it is true. Each record names its source type and links to sources. Inclusion is source-trail context, not endorsement or clinical validation.
