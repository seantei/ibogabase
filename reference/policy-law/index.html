Law and Policy

Ibogaine policy is moving quickly across states, countries, agencies, research programs, and private-sector activity. IbogaBase tracks what a law or policy actually permits, what it does not permit, and what remains federally constrained.

Key Points

  • State interest or funding does not override federal legal status in the United States.
  • Trial authorization, IND activity, and policy study activity should not be described as treatment approval.
  • Country-by-country clinic availability and legal status should be sourced and dated.
  • Policy pages should link to original bills, agency documents, and trial records whenever possible.
