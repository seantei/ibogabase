Respect, sourcing, and reciprocity

Culture and Stewardship

A serious Iboga reference must respect the cultures and communities connected to Iboga. IbogaBase keeps Gabon, Bwiti-linked traditions, conservation, benefit-sharing, and outsider limitations central rather than decorative.

Key Points

  • Bwiti-linked traditions should not be used as branding or aesthetic shorthand.
  • Outsider writing should label its limitations and make room for Gabonese and community voices.
  • Conservation, sourcing ethics, Nagoya Protocol context, reciprocity, and benefit-sharing belong in core coverage.
  • Iboga is not only a biomedical substance or addiction-treatment story.
