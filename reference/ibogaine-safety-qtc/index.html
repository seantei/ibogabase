Ibogaine Safety

Safety is the trust test for any serious Ibogaine resource. IbogaBase keeps cardiac risk, medication interactions, psychiatric exclusions, stimulant and serotonergic risks, electrolyte concerns, mortality literature, and emergency readiness in the foreground.

Key Points

  • QT/QTc prolongation and arrhythmia risk are central concerns in Ibogaine safety discussions.
  • Polypharmacy, CYP2D6 metabolism, stimulants, serotonergic drugs, electrolytes, and underlying cardiac disease can matter.
  • Responsible research settings rely on screening, monitoring, adverse-event reporting, and emergency readiness.
  • IbogaBase does not provide dosing, self-treatment instructions, provider referrals, or individualized medical advice.
