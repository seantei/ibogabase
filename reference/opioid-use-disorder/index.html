Ibogaine and Opioid Use Disorder

Opioid use disorder is one of the most discussed Ibogaine topics. IbogaBase labels the difference between withdrawal-interruption reports, craving changes, longer-term recovery outcomes, relapse-rate claims, and the safety risks that remain central.

Key Points

  • Withdrawal and craving claims should be separated from long-term recovery claims.
  • Observational outcomes can be meaningful but may include selection bias, confounders, and incomplete follow-up.
  • Comparisons with methadone, buprenorphine, naltrexone, and other standards of care need careful framing.
  • No page should imply that Ibogaine cures addiction or is appropriate for a specific person.
