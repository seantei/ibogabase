Emerging claims and uncertainty

Parkinson's, TBI, and Neurology

Interest in Ibogaine-related compounds sometimes extends to Parkinson's disease, traumatic brain injury, neuroplasticity, and neuropsychiatric mechanisms. IbogaBase treats this as an emerging and uncertain area that requires careful source labeling.

Key Points

  • Mechanistic or animal findings are not presented as human clinical proof.
  • Parkinson's-related claims need especially careful distinction between hypothesis, preclinical work, case discussion, and clinical evidence.
  • TBI and neuropsychiatric research is tracked separately from addiction-treatment claims.
  • Safety risks and exclusions still matter even when the condition under discussion is neurological.
