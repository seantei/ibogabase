Listening library and claims

Podcasts and Media

People often discover Iboga and Ibogaine through podcasts, interviews, documentaries, books, and social posts. IbogaBase aims to catalog those sources with short summaries, source labels, claim notes, and links to originals.

Key Points

  • Media entries should distinguish interviews, journalism, personal anecdotes, clinic marketing, documentaries, books, social posts, and peer-reviewed sources.
  • Summaries should be original, brief, and linked to the original source rather than copying transcripts or show notes.
  • High-profile interviews can be useful, but claims still need evidence and safety context.
  • A strong media library helps journalists and newcomers avoid repeating outdated or overstated claims.
